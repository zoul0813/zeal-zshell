ZSHELL LESSON 7 / YOUR BROWSER'S DISKS
====================================

This demo keeps its disk images in browser storage called OPFS.
Completed TF and EEPROM device writes persist automatically in this
browser profile. Your files do not change anyone else's demo.
History and running programs are in memory, not part of those disks.

PAGE CONTROLS
  Reset          Restart the emulator and guest, keeping saved disks
  Unmute         Resume audio if browser autoplay rules suspended it
  Toggle FPS     Show or hide the emulator frame rate
  Update Images  Replace changed saved images with deployed versions
  Clear Data     Restore all configured disks from the deployment

Update Images appears when an image change is detected. Update Images
and Clear Data ask before discarding local changes and guest RAM.
A new deployment does not silently replace your saved disk images.

Only writes that reach a device are covered by automatic persistence.
Programs may still hold unsaved data. ROM/NOR writes stay buffered until
an orderly flush, such as the page's Reset; closing a tab can lose them.
Browser site-data clearing can remove disks. Old IndexedDB saves are not
migrated. There is no Save Data button or automatic memory fallback.
